# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- **Open with Tsuzuri**: a document-style preview beside the editor that updates as you type.
- Edit paragraphs and headings in place, other blocks as Markdown; tick to-dos and add blocks.
- Edit boards, calendars, timelines, charts and forms through their own controls.
- Drag blocks by their ⠿ handle, and drag board cards between columns.
- Boards, calendars, timelines, charts and forms as cards and charts; flowcharts drawn by Tsuzuri; equations typeset with KaTeX.
- Callouts, toggles, columns, covers and `[[links]]` in the preview.
- `/` block menu with Tsuzuri's blocks, table edits, sub-pages and attachments; note names after `[[`.
- Export a note to PDF and choose a theme.
